Pricing

All prices are starting points — change them here anytime. New bookings use the new prices right away; parties already booked keep the price they were quoted. Costs are your estimates and are never shown to customers.

Peak & off-peak start times

Added to (or taken off) the price when a party starts in that window. Use a negative number for a discount.

Vendors

Partner orders & payouts

Orders are created and emailed to the vendor when a family pays. Mark them delivered after the party, then paid out once you’ve paid the vendor.

Emails

Customers automatically get: booking confirmation, payment receipts, a balance reminder before it's due, a past-due notice, and a "see you tomorrow" email. Group/corporate inquiries get a "we got your request" reply. Staff get alerts for new parties, new leads and overdue balances.

Photos

Real photos replace the renderings on the booking page and the search page. The first photo is the big one.

Before you come

Shown on every family's party page, one tip per line. Good things to cover: parking, what to bring (socks, water bottle), outside food and cake rules, where to enter.

Video tour

Questions parents ask

Shown on the booking page and search page, and the chat assistant uses them. Questions without an answer stay hidden. Answers marked “automatic” are written from your prices and policies.
